Known for their comfort, expert fit, and high performance in road and trail running circles, Topo Athletic has proudly announced the appointment of Ashleigh Cook, as Sales Director, U.S. Wholesale. In this position, Cook will lead Topo’s domestic sales team, working strategically with a growing network of retailer accounts and independent sales agents.
